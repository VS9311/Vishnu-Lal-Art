# Vishnu Lal Archive — Content & Curatorial Integrity Rules

These rules consolidate the factual and editorial safeguards established in the current Archive documentation.

## Name
The artist's name is **Vishnu Lal**. Do not introduce alternate spellings.

## Archaeological language
“Archaeological recoveries from an unknown civilization” is a curatorial metaphor, not permission to claim that works are ancient, excavated, from a lost civilization, or have archaeological provenance.

## Artwork facts
Never invent titles, IDs, years, medium, support, dimensions, orientation, availability, provenance or exhibition history. Use supplied/verified catalogue data. If unknown, leave unknown or use a development placeholder.

## Interpretation
Separate objective observation, curatorial interpretation, and artist-stated intention. Do not turn interpretation into artist fact. Do not invent symbolism.

## Biography
Use verified artist material, including approved testimony and Archive material. Do not invent achievements, exhibitions, awards, mentors, influences, dates or quotations. Do not describe the artist as internationally acclaimed or museum-recognized without substantiation and approval.

## Esoteric/literary references
Borges, Kabbalah, Rosicrucianism, mythology and similar traditions may inform internal research, but must not be presented as direct influences unless Vishnu establishes that relationship in documented material.

## Language
Prefer literary, precise, material, restrained and intellectually serious writing. Avoid generic claims such as higher consciousness, energy, vibration, subconscious exploration, ancient secrets, mystical truth, or sacred geometry unless explicitly part of a verified artist statement and appropriate to context.

## Luxury
Communicate luxury through restraint, scarcity, confidence, material seriousness, controlled access and exceptional presentation. Do not manufacture it through fake museum seals, gold gradients, marble textures, exaggerated claims, discount language or countdowns.

## Public/private
The public website is curated. It does not need to expose the complete inventory. Use public/private/archived visibility states and prevent private information from leaking into public pages or search metadata.

## Artist voice
When using first-person copy, preserve Vishnu's actual voice and lived experience. Refine; do not fabricate an artificially academic or mystical persona.

## Editorial test
Before publishing a sentence, ask: **Is this something we know, something Vishnu has said, something we can objectively observe, or an explicitly framed interpretation?** If none apply, do not publish it as fact.
